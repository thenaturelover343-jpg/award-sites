2026-10-04 — skill tightened. Pins unchanged (registry still at gsap 3.15.0,
@gsap/react 2.1.2, lenis 1.3.26, three 0.186.1, fiber 9.8.1, drei 10.7.9).

- Loop: signature is a required step. One sentence, three scales (hero, mid,
  end). Three captions do not count. No extra section just to reach three.
- Firewall: twelve automatic fails, also in `rejects.md`. A hit caps design
  at 5 and creativity at 4.
- `references/seo.md`: one h1, landmarks, a human meta description, JSON-LD
  only for facts already on the page. No invented phone.
- Color: one scheme by default. Both only through `prefers-color-scheme`, ink
  flipped with the ground. Scrim rules are a grade, not a 70% black plate.
- Performance: no `opacity: 0` in base CSS, one preloaded woff2, hero ≤ 2500px,
  reserved image boxes, LCP is text or the poster.
- Accessibility: skip link, 2px focus ring, form errors in `role="alert"`,
  reduced motion is the end frame, targets ≥ 44px, nothing hover-only.
- Directions: Scroll film and Object cinema are gated on a place or a thing
  you turn. Type instrument stays one kinetic line. Each direction has a Never.
- `techniques.md`: the scroll sample no longer animates `object-position` or
  omits the `1ms` duration. That sample contradicted `scroll.md`.
